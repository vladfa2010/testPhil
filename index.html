From hospitality to cultural access

Premium hospitality is no longer only about where you dine, but about what you gain access to.

Private Art Encounters transforms a traditional client dinner into a curated cultural experience — combining private access, contemporary art, gastronomy and discovery.

The programme creates

  • Exclusive access

    Experiences that cannot simply be booked or visited independently.

  • Cultural relevance

    A contemporary programme connecting your brand with art, technology and culture.

  • Premium community

  • A recurring platform

    Not a one-off event, but a recognisable cultural programme throughout the year.

The experience

Each encounter is designed as one continuous experience rather than a screening followed by dinner.

1Taste

Guests are served a dish containing subtle clues — through ingredients, texture, temperature, origin or presentation.

2Interpret

Guests exchange associations and try to anticipate the artwork they are about to experience.

3Discover

The artwork is revealed, transforming the space through media art and projection.

4Continue

The dinner progresses, gradually revealing a curated artistic narrative throughout the evening.

2–3 artistic reveals can be integrated throughout one dinner, creating a natural rhythm without interrupting the social experience.

Gastronomy, media art and nature

An exclusive dining experience where gastronomy, media art, and nature come together.

An immersive journey exploring the relationship between nature and technology through contemporary media art and gastronomy.

Each course offers subtle clues through flavours, textures, and ingredients, inviting guests to discover the connection with a curated artwork revealed throughout the evening.

Through projections, sound, and sensory elements, the space transforms into a living environment, creating an intimate experience where art is not only observed, but felt and discovered.

The element of exclusivity

An experience designed around access, anticipation and discovery

  • Secret locations

    The location can be revealed only 24 / 48 hours before each encounter.

  • Private art access

    Specially selected works, previews or artistic content rarely accessible to the wider public.

  • Unexpected venues

    Each encounter can take place in a different setting across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

  • Curated by CIFRA

    From the artistic narrative and selection of works to the dialogue between art, space and gastronomy.

  • Intimate by design

    Limited to approximately 30–40 guests, preserving the feeling of a private cultural salon.
